CupNest – Tech-Driven Sustainability Solution 🌱☕

CupNest is a tech-powered sustainability platform that educates users about the environmental impact of paper cups and promotes eco-friendly alternatives. With features like an AI chatbot, impact tracking, gamified challenges, and email tips, CupNest makes sustainable living engaging and rewarding. Built with Next.js, Tailwind CSS, and Node.js, it’s your digital companion in reducing disposable cup waste—one sip at a time.
